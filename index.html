a little bit about us

Born from the timeless beauty of Shepstone Gardens, Shepstone Spa is a natural extension of our legacy of elegance and care. Where the gardens offer celebration, the spa offers sanctuary, a serene space to slow down, breathe deeply, and press pause. Every detail has been thoughtfully curated, from our boho chic surroundings to our carefully selected therapists and natural treatments. Whether you are preparing for a wedding, enjoying time with loved ones, or seeking a quiet moment just for yourself, Shepstone Spa is designed to restore balance and renew your spirit. Here, luxury is not just about beauty, it’s about well-being. At Shepstone Spa, leave feeling lighter, calmer, and beautifully renewed.

our timeless treatments

Indulge in the full Shepstone Spa experience. Each treatment is tailored with intention, using high-quality products, expert techniques, and the care of world-class therapists.
